An eight-chapter path

The chapters form one continuous argument. Concrete examples come first; general notation appears only when the proof needs it. Optional technical details are expandable, so the central idea remains visible.

01 18–22 min · Formal foundation

Introduction to Quantum Computation

Begin with the question a complexity statement must answer: which model, which resource, and which success guarantee? This chapter introduces quantum circuits, oracles, and query complexity in that order.

Read lesson 02 18–22 min · Essential foundations

Quantum Computing Basics

Learn only the required toolkit: amplitudes, measurement, interference, multi-qubit registers, and the Hadamard gate.

Read lesson 0312–15 min · Circuit model

Quantum Circuits & Oracles

See why quantum operations must be reversible and how an ordinary function becomes a coherent XOR oracle.

Read lesson 0412–15 min · Promise structure

Simon’s Problem

Use three-bit examples to understand the hidden XOR pairs before introducing the formal promise.

Read lesson 0525–30 min · Quantum algorithm

Simon’s Algorithm

Follow the state transformations and see why interference converts translation symmetry into linear equations orthogonal to the hidden shift.

Read lesson 0625–30 min · Core proof

Why the Algorithm Works

Prove the interference rule first, then use binary Gaussian elimination to recover the secret.

Read lesson 0720–25 min · Lower bound

Why Classical Algorithms Need Exponentially Many Queries

Reduce the classical difficulty to one familiar idea: a collision appears only at the birthday scale.

Read lesson 0810–12 min · Capstone

What the Result Actually Means

State the exponential separation precisely and separate it from claims the proof does not establish.
