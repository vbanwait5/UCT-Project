A short course in quantum query complexity

Simon’s Algorithm
& Quantum Speedups

How can a quantum algorithm recover a hidden pattern while making exponentially fewer queries than any classical randomized algorithm? To answer that question carefully, we will build the model from first principles, derive the algorithm one state at a time, and prove both sides of the separation.

Course map

An eight-chapter path

The chapters form one continuous argument. We first define the mathematical and computational model, then isolate Simon’s hidden-XOR structure, derive the quantum algorithm, and finally prove why bounded-error classical algorithms cannot match its query complexity.

01 18–22 min · Formal foundation

Introduction to Quantum Computation

Begin with the question a complexity statement must answer: which model, which resource, and which success guarantee? This chapter introduces quantum circuits, oracles, and query complexity in that order.

Read lesson 02 25–30 min · Mathematical foundations

Quantum Computing Basics

Develop the mathematics behind the model: amplitudes and measurement, relative phase and interference, tensor products and entanglement, and the gates used in the algorithm.

Read lesson 0320–25 min · Circuit model

Quantum Circuits & Oracles

Turn the state-vector formalism into an algorithmic model by composing local gates and embedding an arbitrary classical function into a reversible quantum oracle.

Read lesson 0418–22 min · Promise structure

Simon’s Problem

Read the hidden-XOR promise algebraically: the fibers of the oracle are precisely the cosets of the two-element subgroup {0,s}.

Read lesson 0525–30 min · Quantum algorithm

Simon’s Algorithm

Follow the state transformations and see why interference converts translation symmetry into linear equations orthogonal to the hidden shift.

Read lesson 0635–40 min · Full proof

Correctness & Query Analysis

Prove the exact sampling distribution, show that random equations reach full rank with high probability, and conclude end-to-end correctness.

Read lesson 0735–40 min · Lower-bound proof

Classical Randomized Lower Bound

Ask what a collision-free classical transcript can reveal, bound adaptive algorithms under a hard distribution, and finish with Yao’s principle.

Read lesson 0820–25 min · Capstone

Implications & Limitations

Place the separation in its proper setting: BQP, relativization, hidden subgroups, and the broader landscape of 15-455.
