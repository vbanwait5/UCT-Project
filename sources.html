Primary research

  • Daniel R. Simon, “On the Power of Quantum Computation”, SIAM Journal on Computing 26(5), 1997.
  • Ethan Bernstein and Umesh Vazirani, “Quantum Complexity Theory”, SIAM Journal on Computing 26(5), 1997.

Course materials

The presentation and complexity-theory terminology were aligned with the 15-455 materials supplied for this project, including notes on deterministic time, probabilistic time, and communication complexity. These materials provide course context; the statement and analysis of Simon’s algorithm are based primarily on the research sources above.

How the sources were used

The site restates the mathematics in its own instructional sequence. Definitions are introduced before use, proofs are expanded into intermediate steps, and the distinction between oracle query complexity and ordinary runtime is kept explicit throughout.

Citation scope

External links are provided for attribution and further reading. They are not required to follow the core course path.
