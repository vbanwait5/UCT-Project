Primary research

Course materials

The structure and terminology of this website are aligned with the 15-455 course notes. In particular, the presentation follows the course’s emphasis on first specifying the computational model and resource being measured, then stating the promise and error convention, and finally proving the algorithmic upper bound and classical lower bound as separate claims.

The supplied notes on deterministic time, probabilistic time, and communication complexity informed this organization and its complexity-theoretic vocabulary. The mathematical statement and analysis of Simon’s algorithm are based primarily on the research sources above.

AI usage

AI-assisted tools were used to help organize the material, strengthen the depth and structure of selected explanations, and turn the project into an interactive website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Veer Banwait developed most of the ideas, remarks, and written explanations; reviewed and edited the final material; and made the final decisions about its mathematical and instructional presentation. AI-generated suggestions were evaluated and revised by the author before being included.
